Basic Plan

Affordable insurance with essential coverage for your vehicle. Perfect for those who need basic protection without any extras.

What's Included in the Basic Plan?

The Basic Plan provides you with the essential insurance coverage to keep your vehicle protected, without any unnecessary frills. It’s ideal for budget-conscious individuals who need peace of mind but don't require the additional features offered by our more advanced plans.

Key Benefits:

  • Essential Coverage: Basic protection against damage, theft, and liability.
  • Email Support: Reach our support team for assistance via email during business hours.
  • Basic Reporting: Receive straightforward reports to keep track of your policy.

Who is this plan for?

The Basic Plan is perfect for:

  • Drivers looking for a cost-effective insurance option.
  • Those who don’t require premium features such as extensive customer support or extra coverage options.
  • Anyone who wants the peace of mind knowing they have essential protection for their vehicle.

Why Choose the Basic Plan?

Choosing the Basic Plan allows you to get the protection you need at an affordable rate. You won’t have to pay for features you don’t need, making it a great option for individuals with tight budgets who still want to protect their vehicle from unforeseen incidents.
