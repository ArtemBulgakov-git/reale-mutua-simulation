What is the coverage provided?

Our insurance plans cover accidents, theft, and damage caused by weather events. We offer both basic and premium plans, tailored to your needs.

How can I get a quote?

To get a personalized quote, simply fill out the form on our "Calculate Insurance" page, and you'll receive a quote based on your details.

How long does it take to get insured?

Once you've completed the form and received a quote, the process is quick! You'll receive your insurance confirmation within minutes via email.

What do I do if I need to file a claim?

If you need to file a claim, simply visit our "Claims" page and follow the steps provided. Our customer service team is available 24/7 to assist you with the process.

Can I cancel my insurance?

Yes, you can cancel your insurance at any time. Please contact our customer support to initiate the cancellation process.
